# Quick Start

Ten minutes from box to listening.

## 1. Power
1. Slide off the battery cover.
2. Set the **battery switch** inside to **Ni-MH** (for the supplied rechargeables) or **ALK** (for alkalines). Use a pen tip.
3. Fit the two AA cells, matching the + / − marks. Replace the cover.
4. **Supplied Ni-MH cells arrive uncharged.** With the scanner **off**, connect the USB cable to a computer port (not a hub) or the supplied mains adapter. The display shows `Charging`, then `Charge Complete`. A full charge of the supplied 2300 mAh cells takes up to 14 hours.

Details: [[Batteries and Charging]]

## 2. Antenna
Push the antenna onto the BNC socket, aligning the slots, and twist clockwise until it locks. **Always fit the antenna before switching on.**

## 3. Switch on
Press and hold the **power/backlight** key for about a second.
- First time? The scanner starts in **Scan Hold** with all banks enabled and Close Call Do-Not-Disturb active.
- Want a different [[Band Plans|band plan]]? Hold `1` (Band Plan 1) or `2` (Band Plan 2) while powering on.

## 4. Volume and squelch
- **Volume:** press the [[Scroll Control]] knob, turn to set (0–15), press again.
- **Squelch:** press `Func`, then press the knob. Turn until the hiss just stops, then press.

Details: [[Volume and Squelch]]

## 5. Hear something straight away
You don't need to program anything to start:
- **[[Service Search]]** — press `Func` then `Srch/Svc`. The scanner hunts through its built-in Marine, Aircraft, CB, HAM, PMR and other service bands.
- **[[Close Call]]** — press `Func` then `Hold`, set *Close Call Mode* to *Close Call Only*. The scanner jumps to strong transmitters nearby.

## 6. Store your first channel
1. Press `Hold`, type a channel number (e.g. `1`), press `Pgm/E`.
2. Choose **Enter Frequency**, type it using `.Clr` as the decimal point (e.g. `145.500`), press `Pgm/E`.
3. Press `.Clr` to back out, then `Scan`.

Details: [[Programming a Channel]]

> [!warning] Before you listen
> Read [[UK Listening Law]]. Owning a scanner is legal; listening to some services is not.
