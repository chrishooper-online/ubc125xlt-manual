# Unboxing and Connections

## In the box
- UBC125XLT scanner with belt clip fitted
- Flexible antenna (BNC)
- 2 × AA rechargeable Ni-MH batteries, 2300 mAh
- USB cable
- AC-to-USB adapter
- Wrist strap

## Antenna
1. Line up the slots on the antenna's connector with the tabs on the scanner's **BNC** socket.
2. Slide it down.
3. Twist the outer ring **clockwise** until it locks.

### External antennas
The BNC socket accepts mobile (car) or outdoor base antennas. Use **50 Ω** coaxial cable:

| Cable run | Use |
|---|---|
| Under ~15 m (50 ft) | RG-58 |
| Over ~15 m (50 ft) | RG-8 low-loss |

A BNC adapter may be needed. A multiband antenna gives the best all-round results; see [[Specifications]] for coverage.

> [!tip] Reception
> If you get interference or electrical noise, move the scanner or antenna away from the source, or change the antenna's angle.

## Earphone / headphones
The **3.5 mm** jack on top takes a mono earphone or stereo headphones. Plugging in mutes the internal speaker. The output is mono, but you'll hear it in both ears on a stereo headset.

> [!warning] Protect your hearing
> Set a comfortable volume on the speaker **before** plugging in, especially with in-ear buds. A sudden squelch opening can be very loud.

## Extension speaker
Plug an external speaker's 3.5 mm plug into the same jack.

> [!danger]
> Never connect the jack's audio output line to a power supply and ground — this can damage the scanner.

## Belt clip
Fixed with Phillips screws; loosen to adjust or remove entirely.

## Care
- Not waterproof — keep out of rain and moisture (and out of kitchens and bathrooms).
- Keep out of direct sun and away from heaters and vents.
