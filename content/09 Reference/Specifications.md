| Frequency | Level |
|---|---|
| 54.050 MHz FM | 320 µV |
| 127.175 MHz AM | 130 µV |
| 161.9875 MHz FM | 100 µV |
| 272.950 MHz AM | 140 µV |
| 406.875 MHz FM | 180 µV |
| 857.150 MHz FM | 280 µV |

These are hundreds of times less sensitive than normal reception — which is why [[Close Call]] only catches nearby transmitters.
