# Volume and Squelch

## Volume (0–15)
1. Press the [[Scroll Control]] knob. The volume level and battery voltage appear.
2. Turn to adjust.
3. Press the knob to set (or wait 10 seconds).

## Squelch (0–15)
Squelch mutes the speaker until a signal is strong enough.

1. Press `Func`, then press the knob. The squelch level and battery voltage appear.
2. Turn until the background hiss **just** stops.
3. Press the knob to set.

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| Stopping on weak, broken or partial signals | Raise squelch |
| Missing weak or distant stations | Lower squelch |
| Stopping with no sound at all | Possibly a birdie — see [[Troubleshooting]] |

> [!note]
> Squelch is also what [[Scanning Stored Channels|scanning]] relies on: the scanner stops wherever squelch opens. Set too low, it stops on noise everywhere.
