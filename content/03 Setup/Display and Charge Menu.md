# Display and Charge Menu

Open with **`Func`** then the **power/backlight** key. Turn the [[Scroll Control]] to choose, `Pgm/E` to select, `.Clr` to exit.

## Set Backlight
| Option | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| Always Off *(default)* | Never on |
| Always On | Always on |
| On with Squelch | On while a signal is received, until the delay ends |
| On with Keypress | On for 10 s after any key |
| Keypress+Squelch | Both of the above |

**Quick toggle:** a plain press of the power key flips the light. If the setting is *Always On*, it switches to *Always Off*, and vice versa.

> [!tip] Battery life
> *Keypress+Squelch* gives you light when you need it without draining the cells.

## Set Contrast
15 levels; the display changes as you scroll. `Pgm/E` to keep.

## Set ChargeTime
1–14 hours (default 14). See [[Batteries and Charging#Charge timer]].
