# Factory Reset

> [!danger] Erases everything
> All channels, tags and settings you've programmed are deleted and **cannot be recovered**. Back up first with [[PC Programming|PC software]] if you can.

1. With the scanner off, **press and hold `2`, `9` and `Hold`**.
2. While holding them, switch the scanner on.
3. `All Memory Clear` appears.

Everything returns to factory defaults **except the [[Band Plans|band plan]]**. The scanner starts in Scan Hold with all banks enabled and Close Call DND active.

> [!note] Pre-loaded scanners
> Some UK retailers sell the scanner pre-programmed with local frequencies. A reset wipes those; keep a backup.
