# Band Plans

The scanner has two sets of default **step sizes** and **modulation** for each frequency range.

## Choosing one
While pressing and holding the power key (~1 second) to switch on, also hold:
- `1` → **Band Plan 1**
- `2` → **Band Plan 2**

The choice survives a [[Factory Reset]].

## The differences that matter

| Range (MHz) | Band Plan 1 | Band Plan 2 |
|---|---|---|
| 25–30.195 (incl. CB 27 MHz) | 5 kHz **FM** | 5 kHz **AM** |
| 84.015–87.295 | 5 kHz FM | **20 kHz with 15 kHz offset**, FM |
| 137–174 | **6.25 kHz** throughout | Mix of **5 / 10 / 12.5 kHz** |
| 450–470 | 6.25 kHz | 10 kHz |

Air band (108–137, 8.33 kHz AM), military air (225–400, 12.5 kHz AM), 400–450 and 470–512 (6.25 kHz FM) and 806–960 (12.5 kHz FM) are the same in both.

> [!tip] Which to use?
> **Band Plan 1** with its 6.25 kHz VHF/UHF steps lands on the narrow 12.5 kHz and 6.25 kHz channel rasters common in the UK and Europe. **Band Plan 2** suits AM CB listening on 27 MHz. Individual channels can always be set manually — see [[Modulation and Step]].
