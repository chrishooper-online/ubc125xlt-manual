# Scroll Control

The knob on top of the scanner.

## Turn it to
- Select channels
- Adjust [[Volume and Squelch|volume and squelch]]
- Change display settings
- Pick characters when [[Text Tags|tagging]]
- Move up/down through menus
- Step channels or frequencies while in Hold
- Resume scanning, or reverse the scan/search direction

## Press it to
- Confirm a setting (same as `Pgm/E` inside a menu)
- Open the volume adjustment
- With `Func` first: open the squelch adjustment

## In Function mode
Turning the knob after `Func` jumps to the **first channel of each bank** — a fast way to move between banks in Hold.
