# Deleting Channels and Banks

## Delete one channel
1. `Hold` → channel number → `Pgm/E`
2. **Delete Channel** → `Pgm/E`
3. At `Confirm Delete?` press `Pgm/E` (or `.Clr` to cancel)

## Clear a whole bank (50 channels)
1. `Hold` → any channel in that bank → `Pgm/E`
2. **Clear Bank** → `Pgm/E`
3. At `Confirm Clear?` press `Pgm/E` (or `.Clr` to cancel)

> [!warning]
> There's no undo. To wipe *everything*, see [[Factory Reset]].

> [!tip] Alternative to deleting
> If you might want a channel again, [[Lockouts|lock it out]] instead.
