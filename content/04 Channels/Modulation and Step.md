# Modulation and Step

## Modulation (AM / FM)
### For a stored channel
`Hold` → channel → `Pgm/E` → **Set Modulation** → AM or FM → `Pgm/E`.

> [!note] Air band is AM only
> On 108–137 MHz you can't choose FM.

### While searching
Hold on a frequency, then **`Func` → `9/Mod`**. The new mode shows for a second. This applies to the whole band in search and [[Close Call]].
- For **CB and HAM** bands it also changes those [[Service Search|service searches]] and is kept after power-off.
- For other bands it **reverts to default** at power-off.

Can also be set from the [[Search Menu]].

## Step size
The gap between frequencies the scanner tries when searching.

Hold on a frequency, then **`Func` → `3/Step`**, turn the knob, `Pgm/E`.

| Band | Steps available |
|---|---|
| Most bands | Default, 5, 6.25, 10, 12.5 kHz |
| Air band | 8.33 or 12.5 kHz |
| 84.015–88 MHz | Adds 20 kHz |

The step applies to search and Close Call; for air band it also changes the air [[Service Search]]. **It resets to default at power-off.**

> [!tip] Air band in the UK and Europe
> European airspace uses 8.33 kHz channel spacing, so leave the air band on **8.33 kHz** to catch every channel.
