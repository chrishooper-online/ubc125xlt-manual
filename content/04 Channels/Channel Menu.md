# Channel Menu

**Open:** `Hold` → channel number → `Pgm/E`
(or `Hold` → turn to channel → `Func` → `Pgm/E`)

Turn the [[Scroll Control]] to choose, `Pgm/E` to select, `.Clr` to go back.

| Item | What it sets | Default | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Enter Frequency | The frequency | — | [[Programming a Channel]] |
| Edit Tag | Name, up to 16 characters | Bank + channel number | [[Text Tags]] |
| Set CTCSS/DCS | Tone squelch | Off | [[CTCSS and DCS]] |
| Set Modulation | AM / FM | From band plan | [[Modulation and Step]] |
| Set Delay | Resume delay −10 to 5 s | 2 s | [[Delay]] |
| Set Priority | Priority On / Off | Off | [[Priority Scanning]] |
| Delete Channel | Erase this channel | — | [[Deleting Channels and Banks]] |
| Clear Bank | Erase all 50 in this bank | — | [[Deleting Channels and Banks]] |
