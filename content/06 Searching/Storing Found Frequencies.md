# Storing Found Frequencies

Found something worth keeping in a search or [[Close Call]]? Save it to a channel.

## From any search or Close Call mode
1. While on the frequency, press **`Pgm/E`**.
2. `Save Frequency` appears with the **first empty channel**.
3. Turn the [[Scroll Control]] to choose another channel if you like.
4. `Pgm/E` to store (or `.Clr` to cancel).

You land in that channel's [[Channel Menu]] — a good moment to add a [[Text Tags|tag]], tone or delay.

## From the Close Call Hits bank, in Scan mode
Scroll to the hit in Scan Hold, then press **`Func` → `Pgm/E`** and follow steps 2–4.

> [!tip]
> If [[CTCSS and DCS|tone search]] is on, note the tone shown when you find the frequency — then set it on the channel to filter out other users.
