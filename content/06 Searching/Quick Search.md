# Quick Search

Search up or down from **any frequency you choose**, without setting limits.

## Start from a typed frequency
1. In Scan or Search, press `Hold`.
2. Type the frequency **with a decimal point** (`.Clr`) so it isn't read as a channel number.
3. Press `Hold` — you're held on it, top line shows `Quick Search`.
4. Press `Hold` again to start searching.

## Start from where you are
- In any Hold mode: turn the [[Scroll Control]] to a frequency, press `Hold`.
- In [[Close Call]] Only, Hold: turn the knob, then `Hold`.

## Controls
| Do | How |
|---|---|
| Reverse or skip | Turn the knob or press `Srch/Svc` |
| Hold / resume | `Hold` |
| Store | `Pgm/E` — [[Storing Found Frequencies]] |
| Lock out | `L/O` once / twice — [[Lockouts]] |
| Leave Quick Search | `Scan` |
