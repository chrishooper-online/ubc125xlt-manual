# Service Search

Searches the scanner's built-in frequency lists — no programming needed.

**Start:** `Func` → `Srch/Svc`

## The 7 service banks
| Key | Service |
|---|---|
| `1` | Emergency |
| `2` | Freenet |
| `3` | PMR |
| `4` | Marine |
| `5` | Aircraft |
| `6` | CB radio |
| `7` | HAM radio |

Toggle each with its number key. At least one must stay on, otherwise `Nothing to Srch`.

> [!warning] Check the law first
> Not every service here is legal to monitor in the UK — see [[UK Listening Law]].

> [!note] Freenet
> Freenet is a German licence-free band (around 149 MHz); unlikely to be busy in the UK.

## While searching
- Top line: service name
- Second line: frequency, ↑/↓, modulation
- Skip a long transmission or reverse: turn the [[Scroll Control]] or `Func` → `Srch/Svc`

## When it stops
| Do | How |
|---|---|
| Hold | `Hold` (turn the knob to step, `Hold` to resume) |
| Store it | `Pgm/E` — see [[Storing Found Frequencies]] |
| Temporary / permanent lockout | `L/O` once / twice — see [[Lockouts]] |
| Change delay, tone search, review lockouts | `Func` → `Pgm/E` — see [[Search Menu]] |

> [!tip]
> Changing air-band step or CB/HAM modulation with [[Modulation and Step|Func+3 / Func+9]] also changes how those service banks search.
