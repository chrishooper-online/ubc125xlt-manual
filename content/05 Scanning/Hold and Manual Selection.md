# Hold and Manual Selection

## Hold on what you're hearing
Press **`Hold`** during Scan or Search. `HOLD` appears. Press `Hold` again (or `Scan`) to resume.

While held, turn the [[Scroll Control]] to step channel by channel (or frequency by frequency in search). After `Func`, turning jumps to the first channel of each bank.

## Go straight to a channel
1. Press `Hold`.
2. Type the channel number.
3. Press `Hold` again.

Useful for keeping watch on one channel during an incident. You can select [[Lockouts|locked-out]] channels this way.

## Monitor any frequency without storing it
1. Press `Hold`.
2. Type the frequency **including the decimal point** (with `.Clr`) — this tells the scanner it's a frequency, not a channel number.
3. Press `Hold`.

You're now in [[Quick Search]] hold on that frequency. Press `Hold` again to search from there, or `Pgm/E` to [[Storing Found Frequencies|store it]].
