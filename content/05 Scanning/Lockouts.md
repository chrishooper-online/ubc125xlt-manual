# Lockouts

Lock out channels or frequencies that are noisy, constant or dull, so scanning and searching skip them.

## Two kinds
| Press `L/O` | Display | Lasts |
|---|---|---|
| Once | `Temporary L/O`, then **TL/O** | Until power-off |
| Twice quickly | `Locked Out`, then **L/O** | Until you unlock it |

## Channels
**While receiving or held:** press `L/O` once or twice. In Scan, scanning carries on from the next channel.

**A specific channel:** `Hold` → channel number → `Hold` → `L/O` once or twice.

**Unlock one:** go to the channel (as above) and press `L/O`; the icon disappears.

**Unlock all** in currently enabled banks: `Hold`, then **press and hold `L/O`** until `Confirm Unlock All Channels?`, then `Pgm/E` (or `.Clr` to cancel).

You can still select a locked-out channel manually. If every channel in a bank is locked out you'll see `All Locked!`.

## Search frequencies
Up to **200**: 100 temporary + 100 permanent.

**Lock out:** while receiving in any search or Close Call, `L/O` once or twice.

> [!note] Shared list
> A frequency locked out in one search mode is locked out in **all** of them — Service, Custom, Quick and Close Call.

**Review or unlock one:** `Func` → `Pgm/E` in a search → *Review Lockouts* → *Review Freq L/O* → scroll → `L/O` to unlock.

**Unlock all:** `Func` → `Pgm/E` → *Review Lockouts* → *Unlock All* → `Pgm/E` to confirm.
*Shortcut:* in any Search Hold, press and hold `L/O` until `Confirm Unlock All Frequencies?`, then `Pgm/E`.

> [!tip] Birdies
> Lock out internally generated "birdies" the same way — see [[Troubleshooting#Birdies]].
